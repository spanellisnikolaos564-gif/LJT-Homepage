I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, where I am supervised by Professor Junxian He. I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024; Professor Junxian He also previously advised me during my undergraduate studies at SJTU.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include:

* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM truthfulness and Interpretability
